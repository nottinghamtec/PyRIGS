PAT information in RIGS is provided on a best-effort basis. Test results shown here may since have been invalidated, or the asset may have been tested again and failed. All assets must still hold a valid, in-date PAT sticker; a test record on RIGS does not replace this requirement. If an asset shows any sign of tampering, is missing a PAT sticker, or otherwise does not seem fit for use, it must not be used out on rigs, regardless of the information presented below.
