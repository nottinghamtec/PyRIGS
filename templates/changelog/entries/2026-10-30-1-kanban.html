The new Kanban page (under the Rigboard menu) shows every rig as a card in a column for where it's up to, from provisional through to paid, so you can see what needs doing at a glance.

See what's changed
  • Drag to change status: drag a rig between Provisional, Confirmed and Booked. Each card's menu does the same, for phones and keyboards.
  • Cancel or invoice in one move: while you're dragging, Cancel event and Create invoice boxes appear at the bottom of the screen. Cancelling can be undone straight away, and creating an invoice gives you a link to it.
  • Split by client: rigs are grouped into Internal (SU accounts), University (non-SU clients with an @nottingham.ac.uk email) and External, with dry hires in their own group at the bottom.
  • Things to chase are flagged: a card with a yellow edge has icons for what's missing, and hovering one explains it. Provisional rigs are flagged until you click the envelope to mark the default response as sent (it's also a box on the event form), and any rig missing basic details (venue, client or start time) is flagged. Confirmed and Booked rigs with no forum thread are flagged, and a rig without a MIC is flagged in red, with a red card and a No MIC badge.
  • Follow the money: once a rig has finished it moves to Awaiting invoice, then Invoiced, then Paid. Paid rigs drop off two weeks after they end. Older rigs that still haven't been invoiced or paid are counted at the bottom of their column, with a link to the full list. Invoicing a rig before it has happened leaves it where it is with an Invoiced pill. You'll only see these columns if you can see invoices, and you can turn them off with the Invoice columns switch to get a single Done column for everything that has finished instead.
  • Enquiries: the column on the left holds placeholders for rigs that have only just been asked about. Add one with the New enquiry button on the Kanban or the home page. They need only a name and a date, and have no N number until you add a client and drag one into Provisional (or use its menu), which makes it a real provisional rig and takes you to its edit page to fill in the rest.
  • Choose how far ahead to look: the board shows the next 6 months by default. Use Showing to switch to the next month, the next year, or everything.
