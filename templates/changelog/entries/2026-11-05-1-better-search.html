The search bar at the top of every page has been rebuilt, so it finds what you meant rather than only exact matches.

  • Best matches first: results are ranked across events, people, venues, assets and training. A match in a name counts for more than one buried in the notes, and events nearer to today come before similar ones from years ago.
  • Forgiving: lights finds lighting, cafe finds Café, and small typos or partial names like Portlnd still find Portland. If a word doesn't appear anywhere, you'll get a Did you mean suggestion.
  • Highlights: the words you searched for are highlighted in each result, with a snippet showing where they matched.
  • Search syntax: use "main stage" for an exact phrase, -cancelled to leave a word out, and marquee or tent for either. IDs like N01234 still jump straight to the top.

The search help has the full details.
