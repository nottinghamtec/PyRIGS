Event items can now be split into sections. A section header is a title-only row, and each section shows a sub-total for the items beneath it. Sections don't change an event's total.

Each section gets its own colour. It's chosen automatically, or you can pick a department colour from the section's colour menu. Printed quotes and invoices show sections (though without the colours).

{% if perms.RIGS.change_event %}

Editing items is also easier:

  • Click a name, price, quantity or description to edit it in place.
  • Duplicate a row with one click, or use Save & add another in the item form.
  • Drag rows by their handle, and they drop where you expect.
  • Undo a deleted item, and get a warning before leaving a page with unsaved changes.
