{% load changelog %} {% changelog_entry author="joebanks" date="2026-11-26" title="Export PAT records to PDF" %}

Pick the assets and tests you want, choose the client organisation and download their PAT records as a PDF, including equipment that isn't in RIGS.

All the changes
  • Use Export Records under PAT, Export on an asset or upload, or Export PAT Records in the command picker
  • Search by asset ID, description, serial number or location, filter by upload or test date, or paste a list of asset IDs
  • For each asset include just the latest test, every test, or only the ones you tick
  • The simple format is one line per test; the detailed format starts each asset on a new page with all of its readings
  • Results for IDs that aren't in RIGS are now kept when a file is uploaded
