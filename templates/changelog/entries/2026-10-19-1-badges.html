You can now earn badges for milestones, leading and crewing events, training and teaching, safety, time on the road, and more. New badges are awarded every hour and appear on your profile. You'll get an email when you earn one, which you can turn off from your profile.

The leaderboard shows how everyone is getting on, and whoever has the most badges holds the Headliner badge.
