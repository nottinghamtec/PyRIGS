RIGS can now send notifications straight to your phone or computer when something happens to a rig you're on, so changes don't slip by unseen. Turn them on from the box on the front page, or from Notifications in your user menu.

All the changes
  • On a rig you're MIC on, you're told when it's cancelled or its date, times, venue, meet time or access time change, and when you're made MIC or taken off it
  • A reminder two hours before the meet time with the venue and access details, and a nudge 20 minutes after it if you haven't checked in
  • The risk assessment reminder and the "you're still checked in" nag now come as notifications too, and tapping the nag takes you straight to checking out
  • When a client authorises a rig, an authorisation request has gone unanswered for three days, or an authorisation email couldn't be delivered, the person who sent it is told
  • You're told when your risk assessment, checklist or power test is approved
  • You're told when training is logged for you and when you meet the requirements for a level
  • Finance are told when a finished rig is waiting to be invoiced, PAT testers when an upload has finished, and admins when users are waiting for approval
  • Choose push or email for each kind of notification on the Notifications page, which also lists your recent ones. Your badge email setting has moved there from your profile
  • On iPhone and iPad, add RIGS to your Home Screen first. The front page box walks you through it and shows how to unblock notifications if they've been turned off
