Upload a results file from the Seaward PAT tester and every test is matched to its asset, so you can see what has been tested, when it's due and why something failed.

All the changes
  • Upload a .sss file under PAT. Results are matched to assets by ID, repeats are skipped and anything that disagrees with what's already recorded is flagged for review
  • Every asset shows its PAT status and results, and each result opens to show the readings next to the limits the test program set, with the reason for any failure
  • Filter the asset list by PAT status, such as Needs PAT for things tested before that are now overdue, plus supplier, collection, dates and sorting
  • Uploads and results appear in Recent Changes and in each asset's history
  • Uploads can be deleted after confirming and giving a reason, and the audit trail keeps a copy of every result
  • Find it from the command picker with PAT Uploads, Assets Needing PAT and Upload PAT Results
